Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on multi-tenancy, billing automation, and payment gateways.

How does multi-tenant isolation work?

Every organization operates in strict logical isolation. All database models carry an `organization_id` foreign key, and all queries are automatically scoped so no organization can ever view or mutate another tenant's data.

How are offline payments handled?

Staff and Admin users can log manual settlements (wire transfers, cheques, cash deposits) against confirmed invoices. Recording payments automatically credits the invoice balance and transitions it to `Paid` status.

Can customers pay invoices online?

Yes! The Customer Portal includes a built-in Razorpay checkout integration with secure HMAC-SHA256 signature verification for instant credit card and digital payment settlements.

What credentials should I use to test the live demo?

The database is pre-seeded with live accounts. Use admin@brightloop.io for the Admin Panel, marcus.staff@brightloop.io for the Staff Workspace, and billing@acmecloud.io for the Customer Portal.
